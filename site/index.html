Reading · Listening

Real-test difficulty

Item counts, lecture lengths and inference questions follow the official specification. Every test passes an automatic check first.

Open source

Open and checkable

Every test, every rule and the checker itself are public. Anyone can see why a test passed, and report anything that feels off.

Speaking

Speaking with transcripts

Your answers are recorded and transcribed by your browser's speech recognition, so you see exactly which words were heard. Better recognition for non-native accents is in progress.

Reading · Listening

Adaptive, like the real test

Reading and Listening send you to an easier or harder second module based on your first-module answers, just as the actual test does.

EN · TR · ES

Explained in your language

The test stays in English. The interface is available in English, Turkish and Spanish, and answer explanations in English and Turkish. More languages come with community help.

GitHub

Built by the community

Write a test, translate the interface or improve the checker. Every contribution passes the same quality gate.
