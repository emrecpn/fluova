# Prompt: Listening section

Copy everything below the line into any capable chatbot. Then paste its answer into the test builder (`generator/index.html`) or save it as `listening.json`.

---

You are writing ORIGINAL practice material in the format of the Listening section of the TOEFL iBT® test (January 2026 format). The audio is generated by text-to-speech from your scripts. Match the real test's structure and difficulty: not harder and not easier.

## Hard rules
- Everything must be original. Never copy ETS or other publishers' material.
- Output **one JSON object only**: no prose and no Markdown fences.
- Use natural spoken English with contractions, hesitations where natural, and indirect answers. Settings are campus and everyday life, plus academic talks on general science, history, psychology or business topics.
- Every question has exactly 4 options, `"answer"` is the 0-based index, and options are similar in length.
- Every question has a `"why"` naming the question type, why the answer is right, and the trap in a wrong option (by letter, A–D in your order).
- ids are unique, use `a-z0-9_` and the prefix `l_`.
- Voices: `"female:us"`, `"male:us"`, `"female:gb"`, `"male:gb"`, `"female:au"`, `"male:au"` or `"neutral"`. Mix accents across the section.

## Task formats
- **Listen and Choose a Response:** one spoken line, then 4 written replies.
  `{"type":"choose_response","id":"l_cr1","audio":[{"text":"…","voice":"female:us"}],"options":[…],"answer":0,"why":"…"}`
  The line is 6–13 words. Use these traps: a reply that echoes a word from the prompt with a different meaning, a reply to a different question type (a time for a *where* question), and a yes/no answer to a *wh-* question. In the upper module, prefer indirect correct replies ("I think the printer on the second floor works" in answer to "Where can I print this?").
- **Conversation** (title must start with `Conversation:`), 2 questions:
  `{"type":"listening","title":"Conversation: …","prompt":"Listen to a conversation between …","audio":[{"text":"…","voice":"female:us"},{"text":"…","voice":"male:gb"}, …],"questions":[2 questions]}`. 65–85 words.
- **Announcement** (title starts with `Announcement:`), one speaker, 50–70 words, 2 questions.
- **Academic Talk** (title starts with `Academic Talk:`), one lecturer, 4 questions: main idea, detail, inference and purpose or function.

## Structure (follow exactly)
```
{"id":"listening","screens":[ ROUTER ],"adaptive":{"routeFrom":[ALL question ids in the router],"threshold":T,"hard":[ UPPER ],"easy":[ LOWER ]}}
```
`T` = about 70% of routeFrom (e.g. 17 of 23).
- **Router:** 9 Choose a Response, 3 Conversations, 2 Announcements, 1 Academic Talk of **200–230 words**.
- **Upper (`hard`):** 8 Choose a Response (indirect replies, idioms), 2 Conversations, 1 Announcement, 2 Academic Talks of **190–250 words each** (never under 180).
- **Lower (`easy`):** 8 Choose a Response (4–7-word lines, direct), 1 Conversation (~60 words), 1 Announcement (~45 words), 1 Academic Talk of 110–140 words.

Before you answer, count the words in every talk and check every rule. Then output the JSON object.
